    <div class="section__head">
      <p class="section__kicker">For allies and departments</p>
      <h1>Allyship</h1>
      <p class="lede">Supporting LGBTQ+ mathematicians is more than a statement of goodwill &mdash; it's a set of practices. Here's where to start.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="grid grid--2">
      <div class="card">
        <h3>For individuals</h3>
        <p>Use people's correct names and pronouns, don't out someone without permission, and step in when you notice exclusionary jokes or comments in your department or lab.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="card">
        <h3>For hiring committees</h3>
        <p>Review job ads and interview processes for barriers that disproportionately affect LGBTQ+ candidates, including how gender and name changes are handled in paperwork.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="card">
        <h3>For department chairs</h3>
        <p>Run a climate survey, publish clear nondiscrimination policies, and make sure health benefits cover gender-affirming care.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="card">
        <h3>For conference organizers</h3>
        <p>Offer gender-neutral restrooms, pronoun options on badges, and a code of conduct with a clear reporting process.</p>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="callout" style="margin-top:2.5rem;">
      <p><strong>Want a workshop for your department?</strong> Reach out through the <a class="text-link" href="{{ '/volunteer/' | relative_url }}">Volunteer</a> page &mdash; several board members give talks on climate and inclusive practice.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
